How this editor works (read once)
  1. Pick a section on the left and change the text in the boxes. English is on the left, Bulgarian on the right.
  2. Your changes are kept automatically in this browser as a draft. Use Preview draft to see them on the real website.
  3. When you are happy, click Download content.js, then replace the file content/content.js in your website folder (or on GitHub) with the downloaded file. Only then do the changes go live.

About security: this editor has no password. It cannot change your live website by itself – it only prepares a file for you to upload, so nobody who finds this page can alter your site. Your website host (GitHub or Cloudflare) is what protects publishing, through your own login there.
